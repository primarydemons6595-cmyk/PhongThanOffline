# Bot continuity, server pacing and chat investigation — 29 October 2026

## Native changes deployed to world 106832

The level-one bot NgocVu7291 was excluded from every candidate while its post-death conservative policy was active: conservative selection capped monsters at its own level, whereas the authored starter monsters begin at level five. The shared policy now retains the existing starter band through level five. Higher-level conservative limits and dangerous-area avoidance remain in force.

The controller distributes decisions across frames in rotating batches, covering up to 128 bots within 18 frames. Movement remains on its separate 100 ms cadence. Chat completion polling runs every 100 ms; configuration, target indexing and status remain bounded by their own cadence. Existing monster indexes are shared instead of rescanning the entire entity population per decision. Periodic saves retain their interval and are staggered across bots; shutdown still saves everyone.

Bots no longer perform client-only quest-marker Lua evaluations or generate their private HUD packets for a nonexistent connection. Full combat snapshots are opt-in via `BotAIgent/performance.ini`, section `Diagnostics`, key `CombatSnapshots`; normal production runs leave them disabled. Historical files in `combat/` are therefore not live telemetry. `MobKills` in status remains a real monster-death counter.

`server_performance.json` reports ten-second windows of game-loop work, world activity, bot decisions, routing, target selection, bot saving, diagnostics, quest markers, player logic and ranking refresh. The engine's target rate is 18 Hz; the `OverBudgetTicks` count measures work exceeding 55.56 ms, not network round-trip latency.
